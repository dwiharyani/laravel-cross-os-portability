CONDITIONAL AGREEMENT

How to read agreement correctly

Harmonized n = 132

Plain-language interpretation

High agreement does not mean high success. In the harmonized subset, most repositories agree because they either pass on all OS or fail on all OS. Only four repositories show parser-backed PASS/FAIL disagreement, so strict cross-OS verdict conflicts are rare in this conditioned subset.

Agreement statistics are conditioned on repositories that reached parser-backed verdicts on all three operating systems. They do not summarize all 921 sampled repositories.
HOW TO READ THE STATISTICS

Three simple rules for interpretation

Effect size, matched discordances, and evidence scope should be interpreted together. A small p-value does not by itself prove a source-code portability defect.

11 / TRANSPARENCY & REPRODUCIBILITY

Read the evidence carefully.

Each visualization provides its denominator, evidence scope, and interpretation limits consistent with the manuscript.

01

Matched design

Each project uses a single frozen commit with observations on Linux, Windows, and macOS. Every run records its outcome and evidence.

02

Six outcome states

PASS_VERIFIED, PASS_UNVERIFIED, FAIL_PARSED, FAIL_RECORDED, NOT_RUN, and INTERRUPTED are kept separate.

03

Tiered inference

Observed differences, parser-backed verdicts, and causal explanations have different evidential strengths. T4 is a bound, not the number of confirmed issues.

04

Selective adjudication

Audits of 75 differential NOT_RUN records and four verdict divergences cannot be generalized into a population-wide root-cause prevalence.
